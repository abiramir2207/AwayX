Program Statuses/Categories

 Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

Below is a brief description of the various program statuses or categories, and what will display for a user in the scorecard.

WIP – Lists only your programs.  No one else can see your WIP programs.

Ready for Approval – Lists yours and your subordinate's programs.

Under Review – Lists only your subordinate's programs.

Waiting for Approval – Lists only your programs.

Pre-Active – Lists your programs, your subordinate's programs and any programs from another position that contains customers/operators/brokers in your market.

Active - Lists your programs, your subordinate's programs and any programs from another position that contains customers/operators/brokers in your market.

Expiring - Lists your programs, your subordinate's programs and any programs from another position that contains customers/operators/brokers in your market.

Expired - Lists your programs, your subordinate's programs and any programs from another position that contains customers/operators/brokers in your market.

Rejected/Lost - Lists your programs, your subordinate's programs and any programs from another position that contains customers/operators/brokers in your market.
