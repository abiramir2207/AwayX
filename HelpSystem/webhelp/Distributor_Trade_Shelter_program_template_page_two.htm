(fields may differ for your implementation)

The Discount Types and whether they are enabled for Item level and Group level are in part conditioned on your selection on the basic info page for Groups.

If you select Lump Sum, the amount is still counted on the margin.

If you select Growth, the discount is tied to the volume; if you select Formula Pricing, the discount is tied to your formula pricing algorithm.

Growth Options

 

Select as many growth options as you want to be included on programs created using this template.

 

Allow multi-rate

Multi-rate Growth pays more for each volume tier attained (for example, Tier one may require selling 10-20 cases of product and award a 10% discount; Tier two may require selling 21-40 cases and award a 15% discount, and so on).

 

Allow single rate

Single rate means the award associated with the tier attained will be awarded for that tier as well as the tiers below it (as contrasted with multi-rate, where the award for a lower tier is the same regardless of how many additional tiers are attained).

 

Allow stairway

Allow stairway provides for growth that is tied to product targets rather than volume tiers.  

For example, in stairway growth, the program might award a discount on product A provided 100 cases of product B are sold first.

 

Allow X of Y

This growth option allows you to tie the discount on one product based on the percentage of growth on another product.

 

Allow volume pool separation

Volume pool separation allows you to condition growth of one product to the discount rate awarded to a different product.

Claim Performance Requirements

(fields may differ for your implementation)

 

If you include Claim Performance Requirements, programs created using this template can include additional requirements for the deal.

For example, a logo must appear on a menu; or a product must display some verbiage, in order for the promotion to be viable.

Program Spending Types

 

If your organization has set up program spending types (for example, Marketing, Retail Display, etc.), indicate the program spending type(s) that may be allowed with programs created based on this template.
