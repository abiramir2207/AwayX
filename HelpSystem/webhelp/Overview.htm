Overview

 Blacksmith Applications  www.blacksmithapps.com

 

FORGESM is designed specifically for the food service industry, to provide both insight and access to the business-critical areas of your organization's trade programs and all that they involve.

Controlled Visibility and Access

With FORGESM, you ensure that only the appropriate level of access is imparted to the different levels within your organization.  A VP has greater visibility to the data than a regional manager, who in turn may have more access than a territory manager.  Users can see only the data that pertains to their hierarchical "branch" within your organization.  You need never worry that sensitive data might be seen by the wrong party.

You control who sees what, and who can effect what (such as program approvals, based on parameters such as incentive profit margins achieved, or simply reporting hierarchy), by setting up and assigning user profiles.

Automated Notifications

Using the automated notification feature, you and your team can be assured of being informed in a timely manner about programs that are ready for approval, approved, or expiring.

Access by Permissions

Those with adequate permissions (which you establish) can view at any time the status of programs.  Only the appropriate people are contacted about approvals and other actions that are tied to a user's permission level.

Projections

Easily see the impact of price changes in commodities to a program's bottom line.

Using FORGESM'S data feeds, you have at your fingertips the tools you need to instantly call up historical price data, review pricing trends, and estimate the probable cost of a program.

Program Templates

If your organization contains multiple business divisions, each with its own method of doing business, you can use FORGESM'S program templates to create rules and enforce security for each user profile that can create or modify programs.  

Within a business division, you may offer several or even many types of promotions.  You can set up as many different templates as you need, to represent each of the promotion types.  Using a program template, the users see only the information fields that pertain to them and the particular promotion type, which eliminates confusion and expedites entry.

FORGESM provides you with the power to monitor, adjust and control the programs (i.e., contracts or promotions) with your distributors and operators from creation, review, notifications and approvals to reporting and analysis.

Controlled Deductions

FORGESM allows you to manage deductions effectively, by letting you organize them, and view them, by customer, dollar amount, days outstanding, by who is assigned to them, and so on.  

With a single click you can change the deductions "view" so that you can prioritize them and determine the best manner of resolution.  Deduction resolution related activities (such as making calls, sending letters, notifying contacts) are automated and tracked.

The deduction related accounting research and activities are supported with the ability to search outstanding invoices, view paid and unpaid billbacks, view scanned checks and other documents.  

You can split deductions to investigate and clear each, and you can use reason codes to indicate whether a deduction requires approval.
