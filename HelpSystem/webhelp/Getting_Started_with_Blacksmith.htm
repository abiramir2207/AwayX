Home Page  

When you log in, the home page displays. The home page allows you to quickly and easily get to any functionality in the application to which you have permissions.

Note: If you have trouble logging in, see your administrator.  For security purposes, your account may become 'locked out", in which case your administrator will have to reset your login, and issue you a new password.

Home page: Left pane (application functions)

The left side or pane of the Home page presents the main functional areas listed below.  Generally speaking, if you have sufficient permissions, you can access everything related to these areas.  It is more likely that you may be able to use some of the functions (such as create and search) and not others (like reassign and commodity costs, or changing settings) depending on your permissions.

Graphical icons are shown next to each functional area in the left pane, and these icons also show in the main toolbar at the top of the Home page.  Thus you can easily associate the icon for Programs with the toolbar icon.  Additionally, if you "hover" over an icon on the top toolbar, descriptive text reminds you of its function.

Home page: Right pane (scorecards)

The right side of the Home page displays several "Scorecard" categories, for Programs, Projects and Billbacks. These Scorecards are a convenient way for you to get an overview of your business situation and then "drill down" to see details.

You can also display a search form for each of the scorecards, by clicking the magnifying glass in the upper right section of the scorecard.  For example, if you click the magnifying glass in the Billbacks scorecard section, a Billbacks search form displays.

The numbers you see in the scorecard (for example, next to "Active") reflect the programs/projects/billbacks associated with you or someone under you in the permission hierarchy.  It does not represent a total count of the items in your entire organization. A red box next to the project's status indicates it is a critical project type.  The numbers you see next to Work in Progress reflect the programs you created (this count does not include other individuals in your hierarchy).  See a description of what each program status or category represents relative to a user's scorecard.  What you see in a scorecard depends on how your organization has set up FORGESM, and on the user profile selections of the user profile whose scorecard is being viewed.
