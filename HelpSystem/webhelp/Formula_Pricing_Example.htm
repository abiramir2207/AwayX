Settings Control Panel > Programs > Settings

OR

Home > Programs > Futures Pricing

 

Example: There are three overages; the product is $34.00/lb.

 

$1 per pound transportation

2% a pound spoilage

5% a pound samples

  

Independent calculation:

+$1 (straight)

+$.68 (2% x 34)

+$1.7 (5% x 34)

 

Compounded calculation:

+1 (straight)

+ .7 ((34 + 1) x2%)

+ .1.785 (((34 + 1) x2%) x 5%)

 

Modifications: An overage to the combined commodity value.

With overages added in:

Milk $3.56 - 30% of product total

Cream Cheese $5.68 - 40% of product total

Electricity $.98 - 30% of product total

 

If there is a modification of 2%, that is added to the weighted averages of the commodities.
