group product assignment details

 Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

Where can I find this in FORGESM?

Settings Control Panel > Users > user profiles > Programs > Approval Method Values

 

The products that have either a green colored or white background have been selected to be part of this group.  If the checkbox is not selected, the product is available to the group but not selected.

The products that have a gray background are in other groups and cannot be selected for this group.

If you deselect a product with a colored or white background, it becomes available for other groups to select.

You create the approval group (Users, approval groups) and then you assign products to the approval groups within user profiles, approval methods, by clicking on the group link.
