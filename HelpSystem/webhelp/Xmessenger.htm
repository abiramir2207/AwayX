Xmessenger

 Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

 

Xmessenger is an "IM", or instant messenging tool that is built in to FORGESM.  Xmessenger lets you communicate or "chat" instantly with other FORGESM users.  

Clicking the Xmessenger icon displays the Xmessenger screen.

Click the [Please select a user] link to display available users with whom you can chat.  The user listing represents users who are currently active and logged in to FORGESM.

Click on the name of the user you want to chat with, and click the "connect to user" button.

Enter your message in the compose message area at the bottom of the Xmessenger window, and click the "send message" button.  Once you click send, your message displays in the message text area.  Prior to clicking send, any message you are working on in the compose message area cannot be seen.  If you decide you don't want to send your message, simply delete it from the compose message area.

The text of both your incoming and outgoing messages display in the message text area. New messages display at the top, and older messages are moved down.

If a message is gray, it belongs to a previous conversation.

When you are finished chatting with a user, click the "x" in the upper right of the Xmessenger window to close the Xmessenger.  If another user wants to chat with you, you will be available and selectable to them as an active user even when the Xmessenger window is closed.
