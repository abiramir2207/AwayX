Password - Change Password from Home or Settings page

 Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

Where can I find this in FORGESM?

Settings Control Panel > Password > change password OR Home > Tools > Password

URL Properties: settings/password_form.cfm?PageTitle=Personal Settings: change password

Use this to change your password.  

As the instructions on the page indicate, your password must be at is at least 8 characters long, and it can contain numbers (0-9) and letters in upper and lower case (A-Z or a-z).

Once you've successfully entered your old password, your new password, and re-entered your new password, click the [Change Password] link.

You will need to login again after you have changed your password.
