The Contract Management Performance Metrics Report shows you the following statistics for the report period:

Top 10 Programs by Volume for this period

Programs Approved by Volume for this period

Programs About to Expire for this period

 

The report period is specified at the top of the report, and links for [print] [email] and [fax] are at the bottom of the report.

 

Columns for each of the report segments include:

Contract Number    Program Title    Volume    Start Period    End Period
