Single Deduction View

 Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

Where can I find this in FORGESM?

Home > Deductions

Single deduction view lets you display the actual deduction record.  Across the top of the deduction record with a red background are the deduction number, description, next scheduled action, status, deduction age in number of days and amount.

Left pane

In the left pane of the single deduction view, the actions you can take are listed (most of these are read-only, i.e. disabled/grayed out until you click the edit link).  The email/view log, fax/view log and print options are enabled when the deduction record is read-only (these are actions that can be taken without affecting the deduction data).

Provided you have permissions, you can click the [click to edit] link, and its label changes from Read only or locked to Edit or unlocked.  With the deduction record editable and unlocked, the other options become enabled provided you have adequate permissions.  

At the bottom of the left pane the next scheduled action displays, with any completed actions (and the date they were completed) listed below it.

Permissions provided, options include:

View

Split

Reassign

Attach Documents

Close

Rebill

Delete

Reject

 

 Search options include:

Programs

Billbacks

Invoices

Adjustments

Customers

 

Right pane

At the top of the right pane of single deduction view are links for [scorecard] and [search result].

Any Reminder, Notes and Calls are listed in the appropriate sections in the right pane.

Finally, the deduction history for the selected deduction display at the bottom of the right pane.  The currently displayed deduction record id is listed but is not an active link: the other related deduction record ids are active links.  Click any of these deduction record links to display them in the single deduction view. This allows you to review all related deduction records in the same single deduction view.
