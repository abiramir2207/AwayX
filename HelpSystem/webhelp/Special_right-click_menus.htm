Special right-click menus

 Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

Special right-click menus have been added to certain pages within the system.

You know a special right-click menu is available if you see a little question mark icon next to your mouse pointer when you are in a section of a page for which additional information is available.

When you click the question mark, the special right-click menu displays.  Using the right-click menu, you can display full details about the item(s) listed, and you can return Home or Logoff.
