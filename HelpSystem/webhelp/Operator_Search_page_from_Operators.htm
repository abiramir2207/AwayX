Operator Search page from Operators

Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

Where can I find this in FORGESM?

Settings Control Panel > Geography > Operators > position/category/priority tab > search operator

OR

Deployment > search operators

URL Properties: Billbacks/search_screens/pm_select_operators.cfm

Select any combination of columns on which to search or just click the [search operators] link to display all entries.

You can enter the End User Name, Doing Business As and City directly, and you can select the State, Location Type, Type of End User (Operator Type), Category, Priority, Territory and Position using the dropdown arrows.  You can also restrict the number of locations included using the dropdown to indicate less than or equal to, greater than or equal to, or simply equal to, and then directly enter the number of locations.

Click the [currently selected operators] triangle to toggle the view to currently selected operators.

When you click the [search operators] link, the results display by Operator Name, Location Type, City State, # of Locations, and Doing Business As.  You can sort by any of these columns by clicking the column header; click the triangle to reverse the sort order of a column that is selected for sorting.

Click the checkbox in the header row of the returned listing to include all operators, or click the checkboxes of the individual operator(s) to select specific operators.  Use your selections in concert with the appropriate link at the bottom to [import selected operators] or [import all returned operators].  Use the [remove all selected operators] link to change your selections.

After you click the import link, the Position/Category/Priority Assign page redisplays showing the operator you selected. You can sort by any of the operator columns, and click the triangle to reverse the sort order.  Columns include Operator Name, Location Type, City, State, # of Locations and  Doing Business As.
