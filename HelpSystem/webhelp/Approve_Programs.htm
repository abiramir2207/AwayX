Approve Programs

 Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

 

When you approve a program, it is moved to Active status.  As part of the program approval, a pop-up displays allowing you to print, email or fax the program information.  

With an Active program displayed, you can click the yellow View Margins button in the upper right to display the Program Profit Analysis window.

Depending on the components of the program, the Program Profit Analysis window may show a product listing with product-level approval checkboxes.  The columns on the Program Profit Analysis window include the Product Description, the Annual volume in Cases (or Stat cases, depending on your selection), the Base Sales, Cash Discount Allowed, Trade Adjustment, Net Sales, the Cost, Operating Income in dollars, Operating Margin in percent, the Target percent and the Approve/Agree checkbox.

If you select the approve checkbox for a product row and save, the next time you display this window the row will say Approved rather than Approve, the checkmark box will be disabled, and there will be a green checkmark icon after the word Approved.

You can display details of the calculations for Allowances, Trade Adjustments, Net Sales, Costs and Margins, by clicking the red or blue amount.  The details display in a pop-up window.  In the Trade Adjustments pop-up, you can display further details of the product rate breakdowns.

 

Click the Lump Sum Balance Information link to expand the view of Lump Sum Balance information, and click it again to collapse the view of Lump Sum information.

Click the [print], [close this window] or [save] links.
