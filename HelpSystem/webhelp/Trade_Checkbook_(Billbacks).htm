Trade Checkbook (Billbacks)

Where can I find this in FORGESM?

Home > Billbacks> Trade Checkbook

For Distributor/Trade Shelter programs, you can see how much a customer has earned versus how much they have billed back.

You can search by customer and then program, or by program and then customer, using the standard search pages.

After you select the customer and program, the trade checkbook information displays.  The Distributor, Program name and id and dates are shown in the upper left.  In the upper right, the percentage of completion for program volume and time is shown in red.  The Credit, Debit and Balance amounts for each item is shown.

You can change your customer and program selections using the three links across the top of the page:

[Choose Another Distributor/program] - If you want to change both the distributor and the program, click this link.

[Choose Another Distributor] - If you have a distributor/program displayed and you click the [Choose Another Distributor] link, you can search for a different distributor for the selected program.

[Choose Another Program] - If you have a distributor/program displayed and you click the [Choose Another Program] link, this allows you to search for another program for the selected distributor.  

Click the Show all Details link in the main section to expand the details of each (and all of the discount components if there are multiples) discount type item.  For each product, the line item transaction information and amounts display (including Invoice#, Case, Pound, Price Paid, Date, etc.) as well as the total Credit amount.

If you want to see details for an individual product,  click the [Details for this Item] to display them.

Click the [Find] link to display a small find box in which you can enter any characters on which to search.  When you click the Find button, the first instance of the search text is highlighted.  To find the next occurrence of the text, click the Find button again. To close the Find box, click the small "x".

Click [Show all Details] link in the component area of the page to display the details of each individual discount type item.  Click [Hide all Details] to collapse the details for a discount item.

Credits, Debits and Balance -

The credits represent the amount earned (exclusive of lump sum, this is the total volume of invoices for the products in the rebate types multiplied by the calculated rate). Debits are created by billbacks.  The balance is calculated by subtracting the debits from the credits and reflects the amount earned that has not been billed back. If the balance is positive (the distributor has earned more than they have billed back), the balance is displayed in green. If the balance is negative (the distributor has billed back more than they have earned) the balance is displayed in red.

Within the details for each individual discount item the total lump sum available to the distributor is shown. Each distributor on a program is eligible for the entire amount of the lump sum. For example, if a program has four distributors and a $1000 lump sum, each distributor will show a $1000 credit.

Billbacks against the lump sum are displayed as debits. The date of the billback, the payable number, billback number (from the distributor) and the total amount claimed are displayed.  In billbacks, growth and regular lump sums are grouped together, and the sum of all lump sums can be paid.  The “debit” for growth lump sums is also shown here. In a correctly paid billback, the lump sum section would show a negative amount and the growth section would show a positive amount.

Additional notes:

Rates for Rebate, Fixed Price, and Formula Price items are calculated nightly. This means that on the day a program is created, the rates may not have been calculated for the distributor/item. In that case, the rate will be shown as $.00.

Credits:  Invoices are gathered for items in the program for the distributor. Any invoices from the beginning of the program date to the end of the program date or the current date (whichever comes first) are shown. Whether the invoice ship date or invoice date qualifies an invoice is set by administrators.  The rate used to calculate the total amount earned is the rate in force on the invoice date. If a program was revised and the rate changed, the item may display two different rates.

Debits - On the right side, debit item transactions display in orange.  Billbacks against the program/distributor have an orange background, and contain information including the billback date, number and amount paid. Only approved or paid billbacks are shown as debits.

The exact volume and dollar amount paid for each item is displayed in the billback details.  

If you click on an individual product line item in the debits section, a window opens showing the product profile including General, Product Alignment and Weight information.

Other component type notes:

Fixed price invoices are gathered using the same criteria as rebate invoices. The rate for items in the fixed price is calculated on a nightly basis.  In fixed and formula price, the invoice price paid minus the program rate is multiplied by the volume to calculate the amount earned: (Invoice Price – Program Cost) * Volume = Amount Earned

Billbacks are dealt with the same way in Rebate, Fixed Price and Formula price discount types.

Formula price debits and credits are calculated like fixed price credits and debits.

For growth, multi rate and single rate components use different algorithms to calculate how much has been earned. Each component in a program is shown separately. Item information and calculations for the component can be displayed by clicking on the [Details for this Component] link.

Single Rate Component: The total volume for all items is calculated in order to determine which growth  tiers have been attained. For example, if the volume of all items is 600 cases, and the tiers are 50 – 200, 201 – 350 and 351 – 500, the third tier was attained.  So for single rate, all items would be multiplied by their third tier rates.  In addition, the distributor would have earned all lump sums for the first, second and third tiers.
In the above example, however, not all the total volume is eligible. Only 451 cases are – between 50 and 500 (inclusive). Also, the program calculates what percentage of the total component volume each item represents. In this case, Item 1 is 66.66% and Item 2 is 33.33% of the total item volume of 600 cases. The contribution percentage is used to determine how much volume for each item is allocated to each tier.

Multi Rate Components: Multi rate components pay the rate for each tier attained, for the volume allocated to each tier.  

Billbacks: In order to calculate growth accurately, the volume of all items must be considered together. For that reason, each billback with growth has only one line item, representing the total amount billed back for all items for growth. Lump sum amounts earned by growth are billed back under lump sums.
