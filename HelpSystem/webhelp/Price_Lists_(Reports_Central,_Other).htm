When you select the Price List link, the Price List Scorecard page displays with three tabs:

Price List, Special Pricing, and Incomplete.

 

Click an entry in the Price List tab to display the details.  The Price List may include columns for Product UPC, Description, Brand, Case Weight Net and Gross, FOB Price in dollars per Pound and Case, and Start and End dates.

Click the Special Pricing tab to see a listing of special price entries.  When you click a special price entry, a page displays with the same columns as for the Price List, with the special prices for the entry you've chosen.

Click the Search Price List link to display a search form in which you can search by Price List ID, Description or Price List type (special, regular, etc.).
