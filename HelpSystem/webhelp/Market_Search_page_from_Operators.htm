Market Search page from Operators

Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

Where can I find this in FORGESM?

Settings Control Panel > Geography > Operators > postal codes tab > market search

URL Properties: search_screens/market_Search.cfm

Select any combination of columns on which to search or just click the [search market] link to display all entries.

Divisions, Regions and State selections display using drop-down arrows, and you can enter County and Market Name directly.

When you click the [search market] link, the results display by Market, Region and Zone.

Click the radio button next to the market row that you want to include, and then click the [import selected records] link.

The Postal Code Assign page redisplays showing the markets you selected.  Click the [postal codes in this market] link next to a market to view the postal codes associated with this market.
