Invoice Search

 Blacksmith Applications, www.blacksmithapps.com

Where can I find this in FORGESM?

Home > Deployment > Invoices

Click the Invoices link to display the Invoice Search page, where you can search for invoices by selecting customer criteria, invoice criteria or both.

Customer criteria include drop-down selections for National Parent Region, National Parent, Line of Business, Division, Region and Territory.  You can also enter a specific dollar value, a Customer PO id, Customer Name and Sold To.

In the Invoice criteria, you can enter a specific invoice number; choose a type of date (Order, Ship, Invoice, Delivery), date operator (greater than or equal to, equal to or less than or equal to) and a specific date to search on.

The Invoice rows that are returned show the Invoice number (you can display invoice details by clicking on the invoice number), Customer PO id and Customer name, Invoice date, Legacy Invoice #, and Total $ of the invoice.  

Sort by any invoice column by clicking the column link; click again to reverse the sort order on a column.

When you click the invoice number to display the invoice details, a window opens showing the invoice #, Legacy invoice #, Customer name, Invoice Date, Customer PO number.  Also included are the Order Date, Ship Date, Delivery Date, and the following invoice detail: Item UPC, Product Description, Order Quantity, Invoice Quantity, Unit Price, Sales Dollars, and Totals.

You can click the [close], [print], [email] or [fax] links from the invoice detail window.
