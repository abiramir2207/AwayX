The Report Wizard offers a library of analytical tools and formatted reports.  For each, determine the dates for your report and additional criteria from the drop down selections.  You then have the option of viewing the report online or downloading its contents as an Excel spreadsheet.  

Contracts - Operator Summary:

For each operator or operator type, this report provides:

  • Volume on contract 

  • Purchases by the operator that are captured via billbacks from the distributors

  • % of contract period expired

  • % of contract volume fulfilled

  • A Performance Index that accounts for the time expired on the contract and the pace of the operator purchases

Contracts -   Product Summary:

For each category, product group, or brand group, this report provides:

  • Summary of the volume on contract

  • The purchases by operators captured via billbacks from the distributors

  • Volumes that are eligible for trade

  • The average pricing discount

  • The depth of the average discount expressed as a % of your FOB price

Payables - Distributor Summary:

For each distributor, distributor group, or network affiliation selected, this report provides a summary of:

  • Volumes captured via billbacks from the distributor

  • Trade eligible volume summary

  • Total billbacks issued, outstanding, and paid during the report period expressed in dollars

Payables -     Deviation Report:

For each distributor, distributor group, or network affiliation selected, this report provides a summary of billback information that has been identified as one of the following deviations:

  • Distributor is not authorized for the contract

  • Delivery dates are not within the active dates of the contract

  • Rates exceeded the expected rate

  • Volume exceeded the expected volume

  • Product not on the contract identified

Trade Program contract volume:

For each Trade Program selected, this report provides a summary of:

  • Eligible volume reported by product category, product group, and/or brand

  • Volume from billbacks for contracts flagged as not eligible for a specific trade program

  • % of purchases claimed against contract billbacks as trade-eligible

Accruals forecasting:

This report uses the information captured from distributor billbacks and forecasts future needs for setting field accruals with:

  • Comparative factors such as metrics (pounds, cases,rebate $'s), period (year-ago, rolling trend), and statistics (% change, aggregate +/-).

  • Report detail options for product (category, brand), distributor (family name, individual houses, distributor types), operator (types, specific operators), and sales area (broker, region, district, territory).

  • Assumption parameters relating to variations from a straight-line forecast, allowing you to map out various scenarios for changes in the pace of the business.
