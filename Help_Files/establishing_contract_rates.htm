Establishing contract rates

Auto apply:

As an alternative to moving your cursor to each rates box down the right side of page and then key-punching the rate for each item, you can apply the same rate to many items with the auto apply feature.  Select the products that share the same rate (either by selecting each or clicking the auto select box in the column header), enter that rate into the rate box and click on "Apply".  To apply different rates to other items, you can follow the same process, but, be sure to un-select the items you have already priced. 

Units of measure:

 As a reminder, be sure to select the appropriate unit of measure (pounds, cases, gallons or liters) for the products and rates you are establishing.
