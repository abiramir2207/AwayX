View payable                   

Summary:

This page displays all of the selections made in creating a payable, its current status, and provides communications tools and history tracking.  The view on the screen is identical to the layout received by those you send email and fax copies to.

Status & approving:

 The broad bar across the top of the page is color-coded to indicate the current status of the payable.  The status changes are triggered either by an action by you, your company, or a date.

email & fax packages:

 This allows you to email or fax the details of a payable to field sales personnel, brokers, internal customers, or distributors.  Please note that not all #application.CompanyName# customers have provided email contacts for their distributors.

history:

 Review all changes that have been made to this payable through its life.

templates:

 Saves the payable you are viewing as a template for future use.
