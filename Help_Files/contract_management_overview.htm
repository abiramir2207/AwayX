Components:

Each contract contains basic information, eligible operators and distributors, and products with corresponding rates.  Additionally, you can select all or some of you brokers and field sales members to communicate with regarding the specifics.

Work in progress:

 Once you have saved the basic information for a contract, it becomes a work in progress that can be reopened and edited at any time.

Pre-Active and Active:

 A work in progress must be approved by someone within your company who has access rights to do so.  Once it is approved, it becomes Preactive, meaning it has been approved but is not yet active as the start date has not arrived.  Once it does, the system will automatically update the status.

Expiring:

 The default setting for flagging contract expiration is 90 days -- once a contract has only 90 days remaining, the system will automatically change the status to Expiring.  As this setting is global to your company, please contact your company representative to discuss revising it.

Archive/Expired:

 Once the contract has reached the end date, it becomes expired and is placed in an archive.

Communication:

 Throughout the process, you have the option of emailing or faxing contracts to your brokers, field sales force, and other internal customers.

Templates:

 These tools allow you to create most or all of a contract and save it for future use in creating additional new contracts.  This is particularly helpful if the contracts you are working with contain many of the same products as it will eliminate time spent selecting those items in the future.

History & Revision Tracking:

Any time a contract is edited, the system captures the changes made and records the date, time, and user who made the changes.  Over time, this provides you with a detailed summary of changes made to each contract.

Additionally, you can elect to load a pre-established template by clicking on "Load from Template" at the bottom of the page.  Selecting the template you wish to use from the available list will automatically populate the contract you are creating with the pre-established information.  When you have completed the Basic Information area, click "Save" to continue.  Once you have, you will be able to return to the contract you have begun creating at any time to complete your work or make revisions.
