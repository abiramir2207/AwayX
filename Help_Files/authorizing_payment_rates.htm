Authorizing payment rates               

rate options:

For each item from the billback, the "authorizer" has the option of: (a) paying the billback rate as is, (b) paying the Expected Rate calculated based on the settings in the control panel, or (c) paying nothing.  

reallocation option:

 Once you have selected a rate for each line item and clicked "Save", a window will open and provide a summary of the amount from the billback, the amount you approved based on the rates you chose to honor, and any balance that is remaining.  You then have a choice of paying only the amount you authorized, or, paying the billback in full but charging the balance against one of the trade programs budgets available to that distributor.  If you do elect to reallocate the balance, a drop down list of trade programs allows you to select the one to charge.

Error-Checking:

 If you do not make a rate choice for one of the rows, the system will not let you authorize the payable as "Approved".  It will highlight the fields that are required before allowing you to proceed.

Approving for Payment:

 Once the rates are authorized, a yellow banner will appear in the "View Payable" area with a selection box allowing you to Approve for payment.
