Product Blaster!

Overview:

To spur new product growth or promote a seasonal item, you may elect to add products and rates to many or all of your existing contracts.  Product Blaster! will add the products you choose at the rates you establish to all of the contracts you select.

Select Products:

 Search for items using the drop down boxes provided, select your items using the check boxes next to each result.

Select contracts:

 Use the contract search tool to locate the contract(s) you wish to add these items to.

enter rates:

 Enter the rebate and net prices per pound and case for each item.

determine over ride:

 Selecting to "over ride" with the Product Blaster! rates will effect the contracts that already include these items, potentially at different rates.  Electing not to over ride will not change the rates that already exist for these items.
