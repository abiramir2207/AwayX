Password:

Choose a password that is at least 8 characters long. It may contain numbers (0-9) and letters in upper and lower case (A-Z or a-z), but we don't recommend the use of spaces or other symbols. Make sure it is difficult for others to guess! 

Expiration Alerts:

 You have the ability to receive alerts via e-mail informing you of the contracts that are about to expire within a window that you select.  You can elect to receive an alert when any contract will expire within 30, 45, 60 and/or 90 days.  Additionally, you can establish a standard message and recipients to send the message to.  If you need to send alerts to more than one address, enter them separated with a comma (,). How the text wraps within the text box, is purely cosmetic. 

e-Mail:

 You have the ability to send copies of your contracts via e-mail and create a custom message that will informing the recipients of the contents of your email.  Note that you should use a string of (#xxxxxx) to indicate contract numbers (note that the "xxxxxxx" string will be replaced by the Contract Number automatically by the system).

Expected Rates:

 To audit the rates any given distributor is charging for net price contracts, this area captures the assumptions you give the system regarding which inbound price to apply for delivered and FOB rates, the duration to apply to calculation, the date to begin calculating, and the range of tolerance expressed as a % +/- when compared to the e-Rate.

Expected volumes:

 To audit the volumes any given distributor is claiming via billbacks, this area captures the assumptions you give the system regarding which how far back in time their purchases and billback volumes should be aggregated for comparison.  Additionally, your settings determine whether or not to "match" the two aggregation periods and establishes the range of tolerance expressed as a % +/- when compared to the e-Volume.
