Payables management overview

Components:

Each payable contains basic information regarding the billback received, the distributor issuing the billback, the contract associated with it, and the products and rates the distributor is seeking.  Additionally, you can select all or some of you brokers and field sales members to communicate with regarding the specifics.

Basic Information:

 This section captures the general information about the billback, such as the total dollar amount, billback number, billback date and delivery period.  Once the Basic Information is saved, the payable is established with a status of "Work in Process".

Billback information:

 The billback information covers the issuing distributor, contract, products, and rates from the billback itself.  Once this information is completed and saved, the payable status changes to Pre-Approved.

Approval and payments:

 Approving a billback for payment allows an authorized user to compare the billback rates to the expected rate, review unauthorized products, and consider timing deviations.  Once the rates are authorized, a payable status of "Approved" is established.  Upon release of payment, the payment reference number, payment date, and payment amount are entered and the payable status changes to "Paid".

Communication:

 Throughout the process, you have the option of emailing or faxing payables to your brokers, field sales force, and other internal customers.

History & Revision Tracking:

Any time a payable is edited, the system captures the changes made and records the date, time, and user who made the changes.  Over time, this provides you with a detailed summary of changes made to each payable.
