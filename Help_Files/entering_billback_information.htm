Entering billback information

Authorized Products:

For each item from the contract you selected, enter the distributor item number, the billback dollar amount, the amount of cases, pounds, gallons, or liters shipped, and the number of operator locations who purchased that item.

Unauthorized products:

 If the billback contains products your company sells that are not on the contract, click on this button, search for and select them, and enter the appropriate information.

Unknown Products:

 The billback may include items that your company does not manufacture and you may be inclined to ignore them.  However, it is likely that the distributor is not aware of their error and the information is capture here to insure a complete record of the billback. 

Error-checking:

 When you have completed entering all of the information, click "Save".  If the total dollar amount you entered does not equal that total billback amount from the Basic Information page, the system will prompt you to correct any errors or omissions.
