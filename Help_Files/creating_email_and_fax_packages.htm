Creating email and fax packages

Electing to create a fax or email packed will automatically send copies to the brokers, sales personnel, or distributors associated with it  The application will "preload" the addresses or fax numbers of the brokers associated.  For email packages, the sender will receive a copy of the message in their inbox that can then be forwarded to others or saved and filed.                    

subject and message body:

To override the system default subject, simply move your cursor to the box and type in the subject heading you would prefer.  In the settings area of the application, you can create your own personal default subject headings and messages.

attachments:

 For email packages, this allows you to provide additional information to the recipient by attaching other files in addition to the #application.CompanyName# document.  Click on browse, find the file you are looking for, and attach it. 

Adding recipients:

 If you need to send the fax or email to additional recipients, add their address or fax number in the "other recipients" box, following the format established.  Be sure to separate each additional recipient with a ",".
