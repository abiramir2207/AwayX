Searching for and selecting brokers

Searching for "all":

By leaving all of the search criteria on the default settings and clicking "Search", you will receive all of the listings in our data base.  A note of caution -- the data base is very large and the number of records you will be requesting may take quite some time to download to you.  It is best to use some of the criteria provided to narrow your search and receive a more targeted result.

Using search strings:

 The empty boxes to the left of several of the search criteria are used for searching based on a string of characters.  The search is not case sensitive and will return any result that contains your string.  For example, if you were searching for Donald's BBQ House and entered "Donald's" in the box, you would receive all of the McDonald's records as well.

When searching for Brokers, search strings can be used against the brokers name and the market area they cover.  

results and selection:

 Select your choice by clicking in the box to the left of each result from your search.  If you want to select all of the results, click on the box at the top of the result area to "auto select" all.  If you need to change a selection, simply "uncheck" the box.

continue searching:

 After you have made some selections, you can continue searching, selecting, and adding to the list of items you are picking.  Click on save only after you have completed finding and selecting everything you need.

adding & revising data:

 From the home page, go to "Profiles", select "Brokers", and then select "Add New".  This will open a page for you to complete that will add the new broker information to the data base.
