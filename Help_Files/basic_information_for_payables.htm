Basic information for payables

Enter general information regarding the payable prior to selecting the billback distributor, adding products and setting the rates from the billback for each item.  The information required for this area is:

Billback  Number:

This is the billback or invoice number from the distributor..

Delivery Period:

From the billback, this is the range of dates the distributor shipped the product they are claiming against.

Billback Date:

This is the date on which the distributor issued the billback and is the date that they will be measuring payment speed against.

Billback amount:

The total dollar amount of a specific billback.
