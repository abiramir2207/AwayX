Basic information for contracts

Enter general information regarding the contract prior to selecting eligible distributors, adding products to the agreement, and setting the rates for each item.  The information required for this area is:

Contract Reference Number:

If you company uses a scheme for tracking agreements, you can enter that unique identifier here.

Contract Title:

Use the title to help in searching and sorting contracts as you build a catalog of agreements.  The title area is particularly helpful when you have multiple agreements with the same operator

Dates:

These are the effective dates of the agreement, with the start date indicating the first day you will honor the prices established.

Trade Eligibility:

Select whether or not volume from this agreement is eligible for distributor trade programs.  If you select yes, you will receive a window with all of your current trade programs to select either all or some of those to signal eligibility.

Discount Type:

A rebate contract pays the distributor a constant rate per case, pound, or gallon for each item.  A net price contract guarantees the distributor a rate per unit that they will use in determining their delivered prices.

Distributor Brand Eligibility:

Indicate whether or not distributor branded products your company manufacturers are eligible for substitution on this agreement.

Volume:

Enter the aggregate amount of pounds, cases, gallons or liters for the contract.

Additionally, you can elect to load a pre-established template by clicking on "Load from Template" at the bottom of the page.  Selecting the template you wish to use from the available list will automatically populate the contract you are creating with the pre-established information.  When you have completed the Basic Information area, click "Save" to continue.  Once you have, you will be able to return to the contract you have begun creating at any time to complete your work or make revisions.
